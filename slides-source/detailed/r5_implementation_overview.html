实施总图：业务轴 × 功能轴，沿七阶段持续共建Implementation map: business × function, delivered in seven stages

业务轴回答“在哪条流程、为谁、改善什么结果”；功能轴回答“哪些能力跨流程重复出现、可以共用”。交点定义任务与 Owner，不是挑一个 Hero 案例。The business axis locates workflows, users and outcomes. The function axis finds reusable capabilities. Their intersection defines tasks and owners—not a single hero case.
