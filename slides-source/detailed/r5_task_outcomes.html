DAY 1 · MD §D1.3.6 · BUSINESS OUTCOMES

平台价值：把业务请求变成可验证的结果Platform value: turn a request into a verifiable outcome

从前面的案例回到平台：理解需求只是起点，业务对象的状态变化才是任务闭环。Connect the cases to the platform: understanding a request starts the task; a verified business-state change closes it.

现场请求：“发现破损库存，不能再被拣走；请安排搬入坏货区。”Warehouse request: “Block damaged stock from picking, then move it into the damage zone.”
