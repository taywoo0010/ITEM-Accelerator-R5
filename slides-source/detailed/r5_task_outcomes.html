DAY 1 · MD §D1.3.6 · ENTERPRISE AI FOUNDATION

企业 AI：贯通知识、能力与业务执行Enterprise AI: connect knowledge, capabilities and action

平台不只是接入大模型：让企业团队能理解业务、构建能力、共享复用，并持续运营。More than model access: understand the business, build capabilities, reuse them and keep them operating.

客户任务示意：“请依据服务 SOP，分析这张工单并准备处理建议。”Illustrative task: “Use our service SOP to analyze this ticket and prepare a resolution.”

01 · KNOWLEDGE

知识散落Scattered knowledge

工单、文档、录屏和经验分散，依据难追溯，更新难同步。Tickets, documents and experience lack consistent sources and updates.

Knowledge Fabric
汇集材料，保留来源Gather evidence and sources

02 · SEMANTICS

业务含义不一致Inconsistent meaning

客户、承诺、例外和规则有不同口径，Prompt 难以稳定承载。Terms, commitments and exceptions vary; prompts lack stable meaning.

Ontology
统一对象、关系与规则Align objects, links and rules

03 · EXECUTION

回答与行动脱节Answers lack action

真实流程需要系统接口、身份权限、审批、异常处理和运行记录。Workflows need APIs, access, approvals, exception handling and logs.

Factory + Runtime
建设能力，受控执行Build and execute with controls

04 · REUSE

成果难以复用One-off delivery

每个场景重新接知识和工具，缺少可发现、可授权的能力资产。Repeated integration lacks discoverable, authorized capability assets.

Marketplace + ItemGPT
发现、组合与使用Discover, combine and use

共用底座，面向不同业务One foundation, many business tasks
知识服务、工单处理、资料分析与审批协作，复用相同平台机制。Knowledge services, tickets, analysis and approvals share platform mechanisms.

长期资产留在企业Long-term assets belong to the enterprise
知识、本体、Skill、运行记录与改进经验持续积累；底层模型可按任务替换。Knowledge, ontologies, skills and feedback accumulate; models remain replaceable.
