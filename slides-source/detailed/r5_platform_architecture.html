DAY 1 · MD §D1.3.1 · 04 PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

七层平台架构：五层核心产品 + 两层开放集成The 7-Layer Architecture: 5 Core Product Layers + 2 Open Integration Layers

第 1 层与第 7 层不是 ITEM 的产品，而是客户已有的基础设施与终端系统。ITEM 的产品与价值集中在第 2–6 层：从语义智能到行业模板的完整 AI 运营内核。Layers 1 and 7 are not ITEM products: they are the customer's existing infrastructure and endpoints. ITEM's core products occupy Layers 2–6, from semantic intelligence to industry templates.
