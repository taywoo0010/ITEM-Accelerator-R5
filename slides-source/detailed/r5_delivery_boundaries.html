DAY 1 · MD §D1.4 · DELIVERY & ACCEPTANCE

落地边界：复用现有能力，以业务结果验收Delivery boundaries: reuse capabilities, verify business outcomes

不是选出一个 Hero 案例；用多组实践对标 ISS，明确可复用能力、流程与组织变化，形成可持续实施路线。The goal is not to pick one hero case. Use multiple practices to shape ISS capabilities, operating changes and a sustainable roadmap.

1 · 先复用，再配置与集成1 · Reuse, configure and integrate

保留已有业务系统和用户入口；盘点模型、知识、Agent、Skill 与接口。能直接用就复用，差异通过配置、适配和必要扩展解决，不先重写平台。Keep existing systems and user entry points. Inventory models, knowledge, agents, skills and interfaces; reuse first, then configure, adapt and extend only real gaps.

2 · 从建议到执行有边界2 · Control the move from advice to action

查询与 Action 都受权限约束；高风险操作须人工确认。区分生成建议、调用成功和业务执行成功；业务状态回写与知识/能力反馈分别管理，可追踪、可回滚。Queries and actions require authorization; risky actions need human approval. Separate advice, tool success and business success; track business writeback and capability feedback independently.

3 · 用独立样本定义上线门槛3 · Set release gates with independent evidence

覆盖正常、异常、边界、缺失信息与权限场景；看高风险失败，不只看平均准确率。保留引用、澄清、拒答、转人工、版本记录与失败分类。Test normal, exceptional, boundary, missing-data and permission cases. Evaluate critical failures, not just average accuracy; retain citations, clarification, refusal, escalation and versioned evidence.

4 · 价值以流程结果和净成本衡量4 · Measure workflow outcomes and net cost

先定基线、统计范围和业务 Owner；比较任务完成、周期、返工、服务承诺与单位成本。计入算力、运维及人工审核；调用量和 Agent 数量不等于价值。Agree a baseline, measurement scope and business owner. Measure completed tasks, cycle time, rework, service promises and unit cost, including compute, maintenance and human review.

本节输出：复用与集成清单、受控行动边界、评测/价值基线、责任人和下一步；不再逐页讨论未定义的产品名称。Output: reuse/integration inventory, action boundaries, quality/value baseline, owners and next steps—not a glossary of undefined product names.
