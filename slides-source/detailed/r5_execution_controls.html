Agent Factory：从业务需求到受控发布Agent Factory: from business intent to governed release

用自然语言建设 Agent，但将测试、权限、审批与运行治理落实到独立的平台机制。Build agents from natural language; enforce testing, access, approval and runtime controls through platform mechanisms.

建设与运行环节Build and run stage平台能力与责任边界Platform capability and responsibility

01 · 说明业务需求01 · Define the brief

明确角色、流程、结果和约束。连接本体、业务规则及上下文；输入输出与完成标准可检查，不把所有知识塞进一段提示词。Define role, workflow, outcome and constraints. Bind ontology, rules and context; make inputs, outputs and completion criteria testable.

02 · Steward 组织能力02 · Steward assembles

组合已有知识、Skill 与工具。形成 Agent 或 Team；主 Agent 分解任务，专业 Agent 分工，保留共享上下文与过程产物。Combine knowledge, skills and tools. Build an Agent or Team with lead/specialist roles, shared context and artifacts.

03 · 验证质量与安全03 · Validate securely

受控参数、端点和凭据下测试。检查正常、异常与权限场景；IAM / RBAC、受限令牌、配额与审计约束访问，关键动作按授权人工确认。Test approved parameters, endpoints and credentials. Check exceptions and access; IAM/RBAC, scoped tokens, quotas and audit govern use and approvals.

04 · 发布与持续运行04 · Publish and operate

发布到 Marketplace，供 ItemGPT 调用。Runtime 支持持久执行；网关管理工具、模型与成本；步骤记录、失败归因和反馈驱动迭代。Publish to Marketplace for ItemGPT. Durable runtime, model/tool gateways, cost attribution and step-level diagnostics support improvement.
